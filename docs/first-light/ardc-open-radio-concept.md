## Project: independently verifiable low-power signals

An app may acknowledge a radio message without a light illuminating, a haptic motor vibrating or anyone noticing. We propose an open, reproducible 802.15.4 laboratory that directly measures this gap using synthetic test-only traffic. Current 6lowStick software simulation does **not** prove on-air delivery or physical action.

**Public outputs:** pinned firmware and equipment manifests; isolated legal/authorized RF bench; independent optical/mechanical/electrical output sensing and calibration; negative tests and honest per-trial denominators; public sanitized fixtures, source, methods and failure logs. LoRa hardware is a later separately authorized go/no-go, not an assumed Thread extension.

**Illustrative scoped $170k *workstream*** (not an ask or funded commitment): $60k technical leadership; $50k embedded/RF support; $20k part-time accessibility/logistics/grant reporting; $20k compatible devices/instruments; $10k calibration/lab; $5k documentation/travel; $5k contingency. The originating $330k [funding case](funding-case.md) has other expenses. **Sponsor overhead, equipment quotes, benefits, contingency allowability and ARDC indirect-cost rules must be reconciled** before this could be an application budget.

**Year-one research gates:** M1–2 authorized bench and quotations; M3–4 calibrated independent sensing; M5–6 First Light *physical* measurement; M7–9 fault-injected controlled Thread RF; M10–12 reproducibility release and separate LoRa decision. No human-subject trial without appropriate approval and consent.
