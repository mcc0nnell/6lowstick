# 6lowStick First Light Laboratory — funding brief

**Draft, not submitted.** Illustrative request: **$275,000 for 12 months** (within a $250,000–$300,000 planning range). This is a budget design, not a vendor quotation, grant award, financial commitment, salary agreement or sponsor endorsement.

## Case for support

6lowStick studies a gap in connected systems: a software command, network send, application acknowledgement, actual light/vibration and human recognition are five separate events. Its current open-source evidence is reproducible but almost entirely modeled, including scripted LoRa and OpenThread POSIX simulated-radio tests. The funded project would establish an independently instrumented, physical low-power signaling lab and test a general-purpose 'glow stick' for local presence, coordination, accessible notifications, sensor conditions and selected safety-relevant research, without claiming an operational emergency-warning service.

Finding friends is a core product motivation. Any real-world friend-presence prototype must be mutual opt-in, revocable, short lived, and resistant to casual tracking. Research controls, not vague privacy marketing, are a deliverable.

## Deliverables and funding gates

- **Quarter 1 — reproducible source baseline and hardware readiness:** publish scoped software evidence, instrument/BOM quotes, design and privacy threat models, signoff for authorized bench use.
- **Quarter 2 — independent physical-output receipt:** actual Thread test send; receiver ACK plus independent light/haptic instrumentation; include failures and traceable denominator.
- **Quarter 3 — physical transport fallback:** direct LoRa point-to-point controlled bench where allowed; compare attempted transport, authenticated application receipts and measured outputs.
- **Quarter 4 — replicable public research kit:** reproducible hardware bill of materials, procedures, anonymized evidence, user-centered consent protocol, accessible demonstrations and documented limitations.

**Stop conditions:** no test without required authority/consent; no real emergency broadcast; no covert tracking; stop or redesign if benchmarks, safety boundaries, accessible participation or budget assumptions fail.

## What sponsors receive

Open Apache-2.0 research artifacts, hardware/evidence methodology, repeatable failure-inclusive testing, transparent budget reporting, and a documented path from simulated receipt claims to instrumented physical outcomes. Results may be negative. The project will not guarantee delivery, life-safety reliability or human comprehension from a software-only baseline.
