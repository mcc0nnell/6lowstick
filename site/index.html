00 / THE QUESTION

A receipt is not a warning.

Networks can report success while a person remains unaware. 6lowStick separates three questions that are too often treated as one.

01 / ATTEMPT

Did the message get through?

A send operation does not establish receipt. The lab records attempted paths and what the simulated receiver acknowledges.

02 / APPLICATION

Did software process it?

An authenticated application receipt can confirm simulated processing. It cannot establish that a physical indicator fired.

03 / PERSON

Did anyone perceive it?

That requires independent actuation measurements and human testing. Neither has been performed in this project.

Our rule: don't promote a successful software receipt into a claim about radios, devices, or people. Read the research guide ↗
